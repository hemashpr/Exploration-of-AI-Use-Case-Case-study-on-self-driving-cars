# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars

# AIM

To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

# THEORY

Self-driving cars use Artificial Intelligence, sensors, and control systems to understand the road environment and perform driving tasks. Cameras, radar, and LiDAR collect information about nearby vehicles, pedestrians, road markings, and obstacles.

Computer vision and machine learning analyse sensor data to identify objects and interpret traffic conditions. Sensor fusion combines information from different sensors to improve the vehicle's understanding of its surroundings. Localization and mapping help estimate the vehicle's position, while path planning selects a suitable route and driving manoeuvre. Control systems then convert these decisions into steering, acceleration, and braking commands.

The vehicle continuously repeats this process as road conditions change. Reliable operation also depends on testing, system redundancy, and safety measures; AI does not guarantee completely autonomous or error-free driving.

# RESULT

The study explains how AI, sensor technologies, path planning, and control systems work together in self-driving cars to detect their surroundings, make driving decisions, and navigate. Their safe operation depends on accurate perception, reliable decision-making, and appropriate safety systems.
